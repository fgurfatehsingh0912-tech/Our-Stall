🎯 Welcome to the Arena!

A giant miniature metropolis has taken over the courtyard! However, a mysterious cloud of red plastic cups has buried our city landmarks. Grab your foam blaster, step up to the line, and help us clear the city while revealing massive treasures!

⚠️ Watch Out for Decoys! Not all cups hide treasure. Some contain nothing but "City Rubble"! Choose your targets wisely to find the real loot.

🕹️ Step-by-Step Rules:

  1. Grab your Gear: Step up to the firing line and trade your game tokens for loaded foam blasters.
  2. Scan the Grid: Target cups are positioned all over our custom buildings. The better the prize, the harder the cup will be to see or hit!
  3. Fire at Will: Take your precise shots. Any cup completely knocked over or displaced counts as a successful hit!
  4. Reveal Your Treasure: Our student field officers will safely lift your targeted cup. Will it be an awesome prize, or just city rubble?
